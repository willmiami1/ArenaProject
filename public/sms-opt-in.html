SMS Opt-In Consent — Destiny Ranch Arena

Destiny Ranch Arena (Destiny Ranch LLC) collects SMS consent only through the web forms on destinyrancharena.com. There is no text-to-join keyword, no kiosk, no chat widget, and no pop-up. The consent step is part of the Create Rider Account form and the Competition Registration form, shown below.

Consent step as it appears on the form

The rider types their mobile number into the Phone Number field. Directly below it is an SMS consent checkbox that is unchecked by default. Only riders who check the box receive texts.

Messages riders receive

  • Upcoming event flyers and registration links
  • Their team numbers after the competition draw is approved
  • A link to follow the live leaderboard

Every message is sent by Destiny Ranch Arena staff, begins with "Destiny Ranch Arena", and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." Riders can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP or unchecking the box in their rider profile.
